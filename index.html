Neuro-Sync Live
Bio-Adaptive AI Audio Entrainment · PWA

"What if your music player could see how you feel and adapt in real-time?"

In a world of constant distraction, existing focus apps play the same "Focus Beats" whether you're stressed, tired, or hyper-alert. Neuro-Sync Live is a Living System — a privacy-first AI that reads your bio-feedback via webcam and dynamically tunes your brainwaves using scientifically proven binaural beats. Stressed → 10Hz Alpha to calm you. Tired → 40Hz Gamma to wake you up.
